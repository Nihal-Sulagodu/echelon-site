Code it. Build it. Pitch it. Race it.

Built for students who like making things work

Echelon is an interschool tech fest run by Bangalore International School in collaboration with Vidyashilp University.

Students in Grades 8–12 form teams, represent their school and compete in hands-on events, from writing code to escape a glass room to racing a drone through an obstacle course.

  • Seven ways to compete

    Coding, game design, data puzzles, pitching, building with e-waste and drone racing.

  • Teams from your school

    Team up with classmates and go up against students from other schools.

  • One morning on campus

    Everything happens at Bangalore International School, from 8:30 am to 1:30 pm.

From sign-up to showtime

Questions, answered

Taking part

Who can take part?

Students in Grades 8–12. Treasure Hunt and Shark Tank are for Grades 9–12; every other event is open to Grades 8–12.

How do we register?

Through the official registration form linked on this page. It opens in a new tab. This website doesn't collect any details itself.

How many teams can one school send?

Most events allow two teams per school. E-Waste Reimagined allows one to four, and Drone Race and Web Designing allow one or two.

How big is a team?

Between two and four students, depending on the event. Game Design teams have two students and Treasure Hunt teams have three. Each event lists its exact team size.

On the day

When and where is it?

On Saturday, 21 November 2026, at Bangalore International School, from 8:30 am to 1:30 pm.

What should we bring?

Game Design teams bring their own laptop with their game software installed, but no game code. Drone Race teams bring their own drone, no bigger than 25 × 25 × 5 cm. Escape Room uses the host school's computers, and E-Waste Reimagined products are built on campus, with power sockets provided.

What does 'Sawubona' mean?

It's a Zulu greeting that means 'I see you'. Shark Tank and Web Designing ideas are built around this theme.
